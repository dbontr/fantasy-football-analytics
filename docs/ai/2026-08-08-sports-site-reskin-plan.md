# Sports-site UI reskin plan

## Objective
Keep SnapCount's current information architecture and beginner-first flows, but make the product read visually like the fantasy section of a major sports website rather than a themed analytics app.

## Design direction
- Concept: compact fantasy sports desk / scoreboard portal.
- Canvas: cool light gray with white content modules.
- Structure: near-black header and section chrome.
- Primary action: strong sports red; dark red hover/pressed state.
- Supporting color: restrained blue for positive/upside information; position colors only where they improve scanning.
- Typography: system sans body with condensed-style display fallbacks, tighter uppercase utility labels, tabular numeric data.
- Surfaces: flat 1px borders, minimal radius, almost no decorative shadows.
- Signature: red scorebar/section rules and ranking-table treatment.
- Motion: restrained hover/focus transitions only.
